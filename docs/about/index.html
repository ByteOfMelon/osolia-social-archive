About this archive

A Mastodon instance exclusively for citizens and residents of the Chiefdom of Osolia.

Osolia Social was a Mastodon server at social.osolia.xyz. It was active from Jan 20, 2025 to Jul 23, 2025 and was shut down in Nov 2026. This site is a static copy of everything that was posted publicly.

  • Only public posts are included. Followers-only posts and direct messages are not.
  • Media and avatars from other servers were not kept, so some boosts and replies point to the original instead.
  • Like, boost and reply counts are frozen as they were on the last day.

Administrator: @admin@social.osolia.xyz
